## 1. Getting started

CALINGA provides public information and online access to two services:

- **AICS** - Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situation
- **Solo Parent** - information and application services for solo parents

The home page also provides announcements, news, municipal information, and links to the available services. Browsing public information does not require an account. An account is required to submit applications and use personal account features.

### Account and application status

These are separate checks:

- **Email verification** confirms that the account owner can access the email address used to register.
- **Account approval** is an MSWD administrator's review of the new account.
- **Application status** is the review result for an individual AICS or Solo Parent application.

An account must be email-verified and approved before its owner can sign in and use protected account features. Application decisions are made separately.

## 2. Resident and beneficiary guide

### 2.1 Create an account

1. Open the CALINGA home page and select **Sign Up**.
2. Enter your full name, email address, password, and password confirmation.
3. Create a password with at least 8 characters and at least one special character.
4. Upload an identity verification document in JPG, PNG, or PDF format. The file must be no larger than 5 MB.
5. Complete the CAPTCHA and select **Sign Up**.
6. When the confirmation dialog appears, open the email account you registered with and follow the activation link.
7. If you do not see the message, check your spam or junk folder.
8. After email activation, wait for MSWD administrator review and account approval.

Use an email address you can access. The activation message is required to verify the account.

### 2.2 Sign in and verify your login

1. On the home page, select **Login**.
2. Enter your registered email and password.
3. If prompted, check your email for the 6-digit one-time password (OTP).
4. Enter the OTP in the floating verification form and select **Verify and Continue**. The code expires after 5 minutes.

If your account is not email-verified, is still awaiting approval, or was not approved, the site will show a message explaining the status. Follow the on-screen guidance or contact MSWD if you need assistance.

### 2.3 Browse public information

Use the home page navigation to open:

- **About:** Municipal history, officials, and social workers.
- **Services:** AICS and Solo Parent service information.
- **Announcements** and **News:** Public updates posted by administrators.
- **Contact:** MSWD office information.

The AICS and Solo Parent service pages describe each program and show general requirements. Requirements may depend on the applicant's situation; review the service information and contact MSWD if you are unsure what to prepare.

### 2.4 Apply for AICS

1. Sign in to your approved account.
2. Open **AICS** from the home page or choose **Apply for AICS**.
3. Review the program description and general requirements on the AICS page.
4. Select **Apply Now** to open the DSWD General Intake Sheet.
5. Complete the beneficiary information and required fields. Provide accurate contact and address details.
6. Add representative information if applicable, family composition, the assistance categories needed, and a clear description of the crisis or need.
7. Review your entries and submit the application.
8. Return to the AICS page or open **My Forms** to review the application status and details.

The AICS page lists general supporting requirements, including a valid ID, barangay or indigency certificate, supporting medical documents for medical cases, proof of income or sworn income statement, and a barangay request or referral letter. Prepare the documents relevant to your case and follow any additional instructions from MSWD.

An approved AICS request may place the account on a cooldown period. If the system shows that applying is locked, it will display the date when another request may be submitted.

### 2.5 Apply for Solo Parent services

1. Sign in to your approved account.
2. Open **Solo Parent** from the home page navigation.
3. Review the program information and eligibility requirements.
4. Select **Apply Now**.
5. Complete the form with accurate personal, contact, address, and family information.
6. Review your answers and submit the application.
7. Return to the Solo Parent page or open **My Forms** to review its status.

The service page lists a valid Solo Parent ID or certification, dependent children's birth certificates, barangay residency certificate, proof of income, and a valid government ID as general requirements. Contact MSWD to confirm which documents apply to your case.

### 2.6 Review submitted forms

1. Sign in and open the account menu beside your name.
2. Select **My Forms**.
3. Choose **AICS Applications** or **Solo Parent Applications**.
4. Review each application's date and status.

Application statuses include:

- **Pending:** Awaiting review.
- **Approved:** Approved by the reviewing office.
- **Declined:** Not approved.

Use the available **View** action on a service page to open application details. Contact MSWD for clarification about a decision or next steps.

### 2.7 Manage your profile and notifications

- Open the account menu and choose **My Profile** to update your name, email, profile picture, or password.
- To change your password, provide your current password and enter and confirm a new password.
- Choose **Notifications** or select the notification bell to review account updates.
- Select **Logout** from the account menu when using a shared device.

## 3. MSWD staff guide

Staff accounts can review and process AICS and Solo Parent applications. Some administration tools are restricted to administrators.

### 3.1 Open the staff dashboard

1. Sign in with your staff account.
2. Open the account menu on the home page.
3. Select **Staff Panel**.

The sidebar links to **AICS Applications**, **Request Tracker**, and **Solo Parent**.

### 3.2 Review AICS applications

1. Open **AICS Applications**.
2. Use the filters for barangay, gender, status, or assistance type when needed.
3. Select **View** to inspect an application.
4. Select **PDF** to open or save its generated PDF.
5. For a pending application, use **Approve** or **Decline** according to MSWD review procedures.

### 3.3 Review Solo Parent applications

1. Open **Solo Parent**.
2. Use the barangay, gender, or status filters to find applications.
3. Select **View** to review application details or **PDF** to open the generated document.
4. Use the available decision action on pending applications.

### 3.4 Use the request tracker

Open **Request Tracker** to review user AICS request cooldown information and the most recent approved AICS application date.

Staff cannot access administrator-only tools such as pending account decisions, locked accounts, reports, announcements, news, and audit logs.

## 4. Administrator guide

Administrators can use the staff application tools and additional account, content, reporting, and security-management features.

### 4.1 Review pending accounts

1. Open the account menu on the home page and select **Admin Panel**.
2. Select **Pending Accounts** in the sidebar.
3. Check the applicant's name, email, identity document, email-verification status, and signup date.
4. Use **Find pending accounts by name or email** to search the list.
5. Select **Show possible same-name accounts only** to narrow the list to pending accounts with exact full-name matches elsewhere.
6. When a match is flagged, open **Review matching accounts** to compare the other account's ID, email, approval status, verification status, and signup date.
7. Use **View ID** to inspect the uploaded identity document.
8. Decide whether to **Approve** or **Reject** the account after completing the required review.

**Important:** A matching name is only a review warning. It does not prove that two accounts belong to the same person; different people can have the same name. Compare the supplied details and identity document before deciding. The name-match check ignores capitalization and leading or trailing spaces, but does not identify spelling variations.

Email verification and administrator approval are separate. Review the verification status shown in the table as part of the account review.

### 4.2 Process service applications

Administrators can process AICS and Solo Parent applications using the same dashboard workflow as staff:

- Filter records to find the applications to review.
- Open **View** to inspect details and **PDF** to view the generated document where available.
- Approve or decline pending applications according to office procedures.

For AICS applications, use the available filters for barangay, gender, status, and assistance type. For Solo Parent applications, use barangay, gender, and status filters.

### 4.3 Review locked accounts

1. Select **Locked Accounts**.
2. Review the account and lock-until information.
3. Select **Unlock** when authorized to restore access.

This tool is available only to administrators.

### 4.4 Review reports

1. Select **Reports**.
2. Review the program summaries, pending/approved/declined counts, and available demographic and program insights.
3. Select **Export AICS CSV** or **Export Solo Parent CSV** to download a summary file for the respective program.

Reports are based on information recorded in submitted applications. Check the labels and totals before reusing exported data.

### 4.5 Manage announcements and news

Use **Announcements** and **News** in the administrator sidebar to manage the public information displayed by the site. Follow the on-screen form and action prompts to add, edit, or remove content. Review dates and text before publishing changes.

### 4.6 Review audit logs and notifications

- Open **Audit Logs** to review recorded administrative events and page access entries.
- Use the notification controls to review system notifications.
- The dashboard's theme button switches between light and dark appearance. The preference is saved in the browser.

## 5. Troubleshooting and account safety

### I did not receive the activation email

- Check the email address used during signup.
- Check spam, junk, and other filtered folders.
- Allow a few minutes for delivery.
- If the email still does not arrive, contact the MSWD office for help.

### I activated my email but cannot sign in

Email activation does not automatically approve the account. Wait for an administrator to review it. If the account is still pending or was rejected, contact MSWD.

### My OTP is invalid or expired

The OTP is 6 digits and expires after 5 minutes. Use the most recent code sent to your registered email. If it has expired, sign in again to request a new code.

### I cannot submit an application

- Confirm that you are signed in and that your account has been approved.
- Complete all fields marked as required.
- Check the AICS cooldown message if applying for AICS.
- Review any error shown on the application page and correct the indicated information.
- Contact MSWD if the problem continues.

### I cannot access an admin feature

Administrator-only tools require an administrator account. Staff accounts can process service applications but cannot manage pending accounts or administrator-only tools. Contact your system administrator if your assigned role appears incorrect.

### Keep your account and applicant information safe

- Use your own email address and provide accurate information.
- Do not share your password or OTP.
- Sign out after using a public or shared computer.
- Upload only the requested identity or supporting documents.
- Contact MSWD through official channels if you suspect unauthorized access or see an unexpected account decision.
